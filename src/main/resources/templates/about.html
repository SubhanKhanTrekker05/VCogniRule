VCogniRule

Rule-Based Early Mental Health Screening System

What is VCogniRule?

VCogniRule is a rule-based expert system designed for early mental health screening. It evaluates user responses across 10 validated clinical instruments using a 4-level forward-chaining inference engine with 150 rules, producing a screening report with traceable findings, comorbid pattern detection, and treatment recommendations based on NICE NG222 clinical guidelines.

Who is it for?

VCogniRule is designed for anyone who may be experiencing mental health concerns and wants a confidential, anonymous screening. It is particularly suited for university students and young adults who may be hesitant to seek in-person help initially. The system does not require login, accounts, or personal information — screenings are completely anonymous.

What does it screen for?

The system screens across 7 clinical domains using 10 validated instruments:

  • Depression & Mood — PHQ-9 (Patient Health Questionnaire-9)
  • Anxiety — GAD-7 (Generalized Anxiety Disorder-7)
  • Safety Check (suicide risk) — C-SSRS (Columbia Suicide Severity Rating Scale)
  • Sleep Quality — PSQI (Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index)
  • Wellbeing — WHO-5 Well-Being Index
  • Stress — PSS-10 (Perceived Stress Scale)
  • Physical Symptoms — PHQ-15 (Patient Health Questionnaire-15)
  • Daily Functioning — WSAS (Work and Social Adjustment Scale)
  • Attention & Focus (ADHD symptoms) — ASRS (Adult ADHD Self-Report Scale)
  • Trauma Response (PTSD symptoms) — PC-PTSD (Primary Care PTSD Screen)

How does it work?

The system follows the classic expert-system architecture with 5 components:

  • Knowledge Base — 150 rules stored in a SQLite database, editable without recompiling
  • Inference Engine — Multi-pass forward-chaining DAG with 4 levels of reasoning
  • User Interface — Web-based screening form and report
  • Knowledge Acquisition Module — Admin panel for adding, editing, and managing rules
  • Explanation Facility — Trace showing which rules fired and why

Is it a diagnosis?

No. VCogniRule is a screening tool, not a diagnostic system. It identifies possible warning signs and recommends professional support. It does not diagnose mental health conditions. The results should be discussed with a qualified mental health professional.

Privacy

Your answers are never saved to the database or any file. While you are using the screening, they are kept temporarily in the server's memory, linked to your browser session, so you can go back from the report and finish a section without re-entering anything. They are deleted when you start a new screening, and automatically when the session expires (after about 30 minutes of inactivity). Only the knowledge base (rules and questions) and admin accounts are stored in the database.

Technology

Built with Java 21, Spring Boot 4.1.2, Thymeleaf, SQLite, and JdbcTemplate. The inference engine is framework-independent plain Java, fully unit-testable in isolation.
