Before you submit

Your Depression & Mood section (PHQ-9) is not complete

We strongly recommend completing it. Without it:

  • Your overall result cannot be determined. The overall result is built from your depression score together with the Safety Check.
  • Combined findings are missed, such as low mood together with anxiety, poor sleep or low wellbeing. Many of the screening rules look for these patterns.
  • PHQ-9 is the most widely used, validated depression questionnaire, so your report is easier to discuss with a doctor or counsellor.
  • It takes about 2 minutes (9 questions).
